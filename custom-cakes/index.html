How it works

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea

    Fill in the form below with the occasion, the date, roughly how many people, and anything you already picture. A napkin sketch is plenty.

  2. 2

    We quote it

    We call or WhatsApp you within one working day with a design, a price and an honest word on what is and isn't possible in the time you have.

    Collected from the bakery or delivered anywhere in Kigali, set up and photographed before we leave.

Your request

Tell us about your cake

The more you tell us here, the better the first quote will be. Nothing is binding — this just starts the conversation.

How much notice do you need?
Two days for a decorated celebration cake, three for photo, character or floral designs, and at least two weeks for a tiered wedding cake. If your date is sooner than that, ask anyway — sometimes we can move things around.
What does a custom cake cost?
Classic birthday cakes start at 25,000 RWF for a 6 inch serving 8–10, and 40,000 RWF for an 8 inch serving 15–20. Premium designs — drip, photo, character and floral — run from 50,000 RWF to 75,000 RWF. Tiered wedding cakes start at 100,000 RWF for one tier. The final price depends on size, decoration and how much handwork the design needs.
Can you copy a cake I saw online?
We can work from a reference, and we will tell you plainly if something in the photo isn't achievable — or isn't edible. What we will not do is pretend a fondant sculpture is a two-day job when it is a five-day one.
Do you cater for allergies?
We can adapt many recipes, but everything is baked in one kitchen handling gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts and sesame, so we cannot promise a trace-free cake. Tell us about the allergy and we will be honest about the risk.

Have a reference photo? Send the form first, then WhatsApp us the picture — we will match it to your request.
